**IMAGE ANNOTATION SAMPLE 01: URBAN STREET SCENE**

**Project type:** Practice annotation

**Task:** Visual scene understanding

**Purpose:** Demonstrate structured observation for multimodal AI training.

**1. SCENE OVERVIEW**

This project demonstrates how visual information can be organized into clear, structured annotations for AI training.

The example focuses on an urban street scene containing pedestrians, vehicles, and buildings. This is a practice template, not an analysis of a verified photograph.

**2. OBJECTS AND SUBJECTS**

**Pedestrians**
- Record visible clothing, approximate position, posture, and observable actions.
- Describe only details that can be seen clearly.

**Vehicles**
- Identify vehicle types and visible colors.
- Record their positions and directions of movement when these can be determined.

**Buildings**
- Describe visible architectural features, entrances, windows, and their positions within the scene.

**Road and surroundings**
- Note visible road markings, pedestrian crossings, road conditions, and nearby objects.

**3. ACTIONS AND INTERACTIONS**

Identify visible actions such as walking, standing, turning, or driving, but only when supported by the source image or video.

Describe interactions precisely. For example, a pedestrian may be standing beside a parked vehicle. Do not assume that the pedestrian owns the vehicle or intends to enter it without supporting evidence.

**4. SPATIAL RELATIONSHIPS**

Record the positions of subjects relative to one another and their surroundings.

Examples:
- A vehicle is positioned behind a pedestrian.
- A building occupies the background across the road.
- Several pedestrians are grouped near a crossing.

**Note:** These are illustrative examples, not observations from an actual photograph.

**5. LIGHTING AND VISUAL CONDITIONS**

When examining an actual image, record:

- **Lighting:** Bright, dim, direct sunlight, or artificial lighting, when identifiable.
- **Weather:** Describe only conditions supported by visible evidence.
- **Visibility:** Note whether subjects are obscured, blurred, or partially outside the frame.
- **Image quality:** Identify blur, low resolution, or other limitations affecting interpretation.

**6. QUALITY-CONTROL CHECKLIST**

- [ ] Every described subject is visible in the source material.
- [ ] Actions are distinguished from assumptions about intentions.
- [ ] Spatial relationships are described clearly.
- [ ] Uncertain details are marked as uncertain rather than guessed.
- [ ] Descriptions are concise, consistent, and relevant to the task.
- [ ] The annotation follows the project's instructions.

**7. SKILLS DEMONSTRATED**

- Visual observation
- Structured description
- Spatial reasoning
- Attention to detail
- Quality control
- Evidence-based interpretation

**8. PROJECT STATUS**

**Status:** Practice portfolio in progress.

This document demonstrates an annotation approach. It is not a completed annotation of a specific image. Actual annotations should be based on real source material and checked against the visual evidence.
