**IMAGE ANNOTATION SAMPLE 02: RURAL STREET SCENE**

**Project type:** Practice annotation based on a photograph

**Task:** Visual scene understanding

**1. SCENE DESCRIPTION**

The image shows a woman and two children walking along a road in an area that appears rural or semi-rural. The sky contains dark, heavy-looking clouds, suggesting that rain may be approaching.

**2. SUBJECTS AND ACTIONS**

- **Adult subject:** A woman is visible walking along the road.
- **Other subjects:** Two children are also visible walking.
- **Observed action:** The three subjects appear to be moving along the same route.
- **Unknown information:** Their destination and purpose for travelling cannot be determined from the image alone.

**3. ENVIRONMENT AND WEATHER**

- **Sky:** The clouds appear dark and heavy.
- **Possible weather conditions:** The sky may indicate approaching rain, but rainfall cannot be confirmed from the cloud appearance alone.
- **Surroundings:** The location appears rural or semi-rural based on the visible environment.

**4. SPATIAL RELATIONSHIPS**

The woman and two children appear together on the road. Their relative positions should be described in more detail only after checking the photograph closely.

**5. UNCERTAINTY AND ACCURACY**

The description distinguishes visible evidence from interpretation. The subjects' actions are observable, while their destination and the certainty of upcoming rain remain unknown.

**6. SKILLS DEMONSTRATED**

- Visual observation
- Scene interpretation
- Action recognition
- Environmental analysis
- Evidence-based reasoning
- Avoiding unsupported assumptions

**7. PROJECT STATUS**

Practice annotation completed as a learning exercise. This sample demonstrates a structured approach to describing visual content for multimodal AI systems.
