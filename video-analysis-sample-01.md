**VIDEO ANALYSIS SAMPLE 01**

**Project type:** Practice video annotation  
**Task:** Action recognition and temporal analysis

**1. VIDEO SUMMARY**

This sample demonstrates how to describe actions in a video using timestamps and observable evidence.

**2. TIMESTAMPED EVENTS**

- **00:00–00:03:** Observe and describe the opening scene.
- **00:03–00:06:** Identify any visible movement or change.
- **00:06–00:10:** Describe the next observable action or scene transition.

*Note: These are placeholder timestamps, not observations from an actual video.*

**3. SUBJECTS AND ACTIONS**

Record the visible subjects, their movements, and any interactions. Avoid guessing their intentions.

**4. SCENE CHANGES**

Note changes in camera angle, lighting, background, or subject position, including when each change occurs.

**5. ACCURACY AND UNCERTAINTY**

Separate directly observable actions from interpretations. Mark unclear events as uncertain rather than inventing details.

**6. SKILLS DEMONSTRATED**

- Temporal event annotation
- Action recognition
- Scene-change detection
- Structured descriptions
- Evidence-based reasoning
